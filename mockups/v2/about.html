ABOUT

Built for speed, privacy, and craft.

astraa is a fast, local-first collection of browser utilities. No servers, no sign-ups, no accounts — every tool runs entirely on your machine, and nothing you type or paste ever leaves it.

Privacy is the foundation. Every tool is client-side by design. There is no backend to send your data to, no analytics watching what you do, and no request that quietly carries your input off the page. What you paste in stays in your browser, where it belongs.

The design is deliberate. astraa is monochrome and minimal on purpose — premium restraint over decoration. No gradients competing for attention, no noise, no clutter. Just the work, presented clearly, so the tool gets out of your way.

The name has intent. अस्त्र (astra) is Sanskrit for a weapon or instrument — a tool wielded with purpose, never idly. Each utility here is meant to be exactly that: precise, ready, and answerable only to you. अस्त्र at your command.

Built on solid ground. astraa is made with Next.js and React, runs entirely in the browser, and is open source — free to read, fork, and trust.
